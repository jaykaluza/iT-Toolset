Privacy policy

My Connections, by Nesiam.com. Last updated 21 November 2026.

My Connections shows which programs on your PC are connected to the internet. Everything it learns stays on your PC. We have no servers, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and we collect nothing about you.

What the app reads on your PC

  • The list of network connections and the programs that own them.
  • Details of those programs (name, publisher, file location, Windows Firewall rules).
  • The titles and addresses of your open browser tabs, and recent browser history, only to show which website each browser connection belongs to.

This is shown to you in the app and, if you use the logging feature, saved in a local file on your PC. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app sends over the network

  • Name lookups. To show a name instead of a number, the app asks your PC's normal DNS service who an address belongs to. This is an ordinary lookup of the kind every program makes. The addresses looked up are the ones already connected to your PC.
  • "Is this the original app?" check. Only when you hover over the app's own name, it asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the published fingerprint of its own version, to compare with the file you are running. Nothing about you or your PC is sent.

Nothing is sent to Nesiam.com.

Changes the app can make

When you press Block or End process, the app adds a Windows Firewall rule or stops that program. It does this only when you ask, and records it in a local activity log.

Children, sharing and selling data

We do not collect data, so we cannot share or sell it.
