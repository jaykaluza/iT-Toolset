Upgrade capture with Npcap and scapy

My Network already works without these. Adding them lets it read complete network frames instead of starting at the IP layer.

Now (built into Windows)With Npcap and scapy
Ethernet headerNot captured. MACs are looked up from the ARP tableThe real source and destination MAC of every frame
ARP trafficInvisibleShown, and devices are found as soon as they speak
Incoming packetsWindows Firewall can hide themRead underneath the firewall
AdministratorNeeded at every startNot needed with Npcap's default options

What it won't change: traffic between two other devices still can't be seen on a normal switch or Wi-Fi, and IPv6 isn't drawn yet.

What you're adding: Npcap is a network driver from the Nmap project that stays on this PC until you uninstall it. It's free for personal use; check its licence for business use. scapy is a Python library.
